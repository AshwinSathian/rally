Rally

Table tennis you play with one finger or a mouse.

Match

First to 11 against the computer. Pick who you play.

How to play
  • Drag anywhere on the screenMove the mouse to move your paddle, and tapclick to serve. Arrow keys and Space work too.
  • Where the ball meets your paddle decides where it lands. The middle sends it to the middle, and the rounded tip at either end sends it off the side.
  • Your serve lands on the side of the table you serve from. Flick sideways as you serve to push it wider.
  • Push forward as you hit for pace. Move sideways as you hit and the ball curves.
  • In a match the ball has to land on the table. Serve changes every two points, and every point from 10–10.
